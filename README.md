# Sciar Tips

- [Live website](https://babatomiwa01.github.io/sciar-tips/)
- [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Babatomiwa01/sciar-tips)

How can Halloran’s staff see which of their working hours count toward their weekly tips, raise a concern anonymously, and keep a record of it?

## Part 1 · The people

**Aoife, inside Sciar.** “Make it work on a phone” (`messages/04-aoife.md`). She needs staff to be able to use Sciar Tips on their phones, so the staff view fits a narrow screen.

**Rafael, outside Sciar.** “i dont understand how they count it” (`messages/01-rafael.md`). He needs to see how his hours affect his payout. The page shows which hours count and how his points translate into euros.

**Language.** Portuguese is Rafael’s first language, so the page explains the 11 p.m. cutoff in plain English beside his hours.
